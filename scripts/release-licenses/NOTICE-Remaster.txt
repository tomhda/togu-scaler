TOGU SCALER converts the published FP16 dynamic-size ONNX to FP32 with a
fixed 512x512 tile. The learned weights are not retrained.
Architecture: DRUNet/UNetRes from KAIR (https://github.com/cszn/KAIR), MIT.
Training targets: SCUNet GAN + Unsharp Mask, per the author's model card.
Native-resolution compression artifact removal and detail correction.
Single-frame inference; SDR only. No temporal consistency guarantee.
